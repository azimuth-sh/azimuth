### Requirement: late-return-owes-a-fine
A book returned after its due date owes a fine for each day it is late.

#### Scenario: late-return-is-fined
A book comes back three days after its due date.
The desk records a fine of three days.

#### Scenario: on-time-return-is-free
A book comes back on its due date or before it.
The desk records no fine.
