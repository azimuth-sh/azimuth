# Verification workflow

The account declares expectations; a Run records execution facts; independent reviews assess what those facts establish. Validation makes relationships and gaps visible without deciding a Requirement.

For the intended assurance control plane, harness boundaries, decision handling and production maintenance loop, see [intended-architecture.md](intended-architecture.md). That document describes direction rather than current executable contracts.

## Validate the exact account

Use `azimuth validate` for accepted state and `azimuth change validate <change>` for the prospective state. Project assessments accept a catalog and complete revision-pinned workset directly. The tool derives repository ownership and mounts; authors do not maintain projected workspace files or manually qualify manifests.

Validation separates structural validity, evidence completeness and acceptance readiness. A well-formed account can remain incomplete or unresolved. Pending reviews are obligations, not malformed declarations. No structural report proves execution, reviewer independence, deployed enforcement or acceptance.

## Select and execute

Execution requirements belong to Checks; a small policy routes generic execution families to explicitly configured capabilities. Local and CI environments can use the same policy with their own exact Verification Targets. Azimuth generates concrete selection rather than requiring a list of every Check ID. Missing declarations, incompatible inputs and unrouted Checks stay visible.

One bounded Run has one exact Verification Target and one semantic Plan. It may coordinate multiple adapters while retaining their individual bounded execution accounts and provenance. Source, artifact and deployed Verification Targets remain distinct. Build and deployment provenance can explain their relationship without treating Check Results as interchangeable.

A Check's Scenarios derive from authored Check-to-Scenario bindings unless an explicit subset is requested. Units default to one `whole` unit; finer planned work may be generated by a package or supplied explicitly. A complete whole unit does not imply complete endpoint coverage.

## Review incrementally

`azimuth assurance review-input` prepares a target-specific, fingerprinted packet containing authored definitions, dependencies, relevant exact-target Check Results, existing review assessments and gaps. It creates no reviewer identity, decision, rationale or approval. Reviewers retain responsibility for method credibility, applicability and Requirement conclusions.

Method Qualification can precede execution. Applicability Decisions follow the Check Results they assess. A Requirement Judgment can record an unresolved conclusion while work continues. Ordinary reruns do not require method requalification; changed relevant definitions, inputs or credible-method conditions do. Different Verification Targets and obsolete execution corrections remain distinct.

## Inspect state and resolve gaps

The ledger retains immutable Runs and reviews under explicit authority selection. It computes exact-Verification-Target Assurance State. Record assessment and Requirement conclusion are separate: an accepted, current Judgment may conclude unresolved. No Check success creates an approved review, and a supported state does not archive or accept a change automatically.

The working loop is author, validate, execute, review and resolve gaps. Validate again after integrating reviews and before explicit acceptance. Decision Policies and challenge scheduling for compact accounts remain deferred.
