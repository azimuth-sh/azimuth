# Design: declared-surfaces-and-obligations

## Areas locate architectural participation

The local workspace reuses federation's stable area-and-mount vocabulary. A source relation obtains
its area from its typed federated identity when present and otherwise from the longest containing
local mount. `Realizes` and `Covers` tags remain keyed only by claim identity.

Areas describe architectural ownership and placement. They are not spec domains, deployable
components, evidence scopes or test locations.

## Surfaces derive complete site domains

A surface has one or more contributions. Each contribution binds an area mount to an extractor
enumerator such as `next-routes`. The enumerator reads the same build artifact that creates the
technical population. Every contribution must produce its own identity-bearing witness; one
successful contribution cannot conceal another contribution's failure.

A site-domain claim names a surface with `Over:`. Every independently enumerated member must
discharge that claim. Tagged behavioral sites remain transitional members for same-id surfaces
until code-consuming enumerators exist for their ecosystems.

## Area obligations require participation, not universal discharge

An optional realization obligation names a standard or critical behavioral claim and one or more
areas. Each area must contain at least one honest realization. This is existential within each
area, unlike a surface invariant's universal requirement over every member.

The obligation carries no role label. A short role vocabulary was rejected because names such as
`projection`, `transport` and `presentation` would add ceremony without proving semantic
contribution. The agent judgment instead reads every obligated realization and states how it
contributes to the predicate.

## Evidence remains orthogonal

Architectural participation does not imply one test per area. Evidence remains governed by the
verification plan's strength, scope, quantification and oracle. One composed test may exercise
several areas, while a risky mechanism may deserve independent component evidence.

## Freshness and failure behavior

Surface contributions, their area mounts, enumeration witnesses and realization obligations enter
the affected claim's judgment fingerprint. Their ordering does not: reordering set-like JSON arrays
cannot expire a semantic judgment.

Missing `Over:`, unknown surfaces, missing contribution witnesses, undischarge members, dangling
obligations and absent obligated-area realizations fail closed as distinct findings. Workspace
parsers reject unknown fields, duplicate identities, unknown areas or mounts and paths capable of
escaping the repository before extraction.

## Residue

The first extractor-backed surface kind is `next-routes`. ASP.NET endpoints, broker consumers,
nested Next applications within one mount and federated surface assembly remain outside this
change. The machine proves completeness inside accepted surface assignments; it cannot infer that
no undeclared application belongs to the semantic population.
