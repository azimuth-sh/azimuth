# Proposal reference

`proposal.md` begins with `# Change: <title>` and `Status: proposed`. It describes the present problem, observable outcome, in-scope and excluded work, exact affected `<spec-id>#<claim-id>` identities, originating exploration decisions and inspectable completion conditions.

When accepted intent does not change, declare `Intent delta: none` and a non-empty `Because:` reason after status. Do not combine that declaration with delta files. Proposal approval is distinct from exploration approval and implementation authorization.
