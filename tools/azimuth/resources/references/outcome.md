# Outcome reference

`outcome.md` records what the completed change actually established. State delivered behavior, accepted departures from proposal or design, engineering verification performed, unperformed checks, residual risks and rollout-dependent conditions.

Do not manufacture production exposure, observations or evidence. Finalization requires current facets to describe what exists and every completion condition to have an honest disposition. Archival preserves the complete decision record.

When a change adds non-routine Requirements and no locally authored Requirement Status exists, the Requirement is ratified by two records committed in the change: an accepted Requirement Review for each Requirement under `reviews/` (sealed native review JSON from `review-compile`, with the packets needed to recompile it), and a human acceptance decision in `acceptance.json` beside `outcome.md`: `format` `azimuth-change-acceptance`, `version` 1, `accepted_by`, `accepted_on` (YYYY-MM-DD), the accepted `requirements` this change adds, and a non-empty `basis`. Finalization assesses every committed review against the merged model; a review that is stale, rejected, missing or invalid ratifies nothing and the `missing-requirement-assurance` finding stays. The acceptance alone waives nothing, and an acceptance with its review clears only `missing-requirement-assurance` for those Requirements. The finalization receipt lists the ratifying review of each accepted Requirement. An agent never writes `acceptance.json` on its own initiative; it records a decision the accountable human has stated.
